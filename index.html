📜 Rules

  • Rounds: a new round every 5 minutes, back-to-back, forever. Guesses are accepted only in the first 180 seconds of a round.
  • Free guess: 1 per wallet per round, requires holding an Agent ID (checked on-chain). Extra guesses cost 0.001 BNB each — anyone may buy them, no limit.
  • Winning: after the round ends, anyone may settle it. The answer is the Chainlink BTC/USD price. The 3 closest guesses win; ties go to the earlier guess. Winning slots are per guess — one sharp player can take two slots.
  • Weights follow the player count: with 2 players the top 2 guesses split 60/40; with 3+ players the top 3 split 50/30/20.
  • Solo rounds: if only one player joins a round, there is no winner — every paid guess is refunded in full via Claim.
  • Pot: all paid guesses of the round. 85% to the winners, 15% to the treasury (paid at settle).
  • SEER emission: each settled round with 2+ players also pays out 2,000,000 SEER to its winners in the same weights — for the first 250 such rounds (500M SEER total). SEER supply is fixed at 1 billion; the contract holds the 500M winner allocation, 400M is reserved for liquidity and 100M as reserve, both with the treasury.
  • Fair by construction: no admin functions, no owner, no randomness, no upgrades. The contract cannot be changed. Plain BNB transfers to it revert.
